## The recipient journey

### Why me?

Answered in the first screen through direct address, identity, company, portrait, and a thesis derived from Mark’s appraisal-trained professional history.

### Why this?

Answered by the visible gap between conventional credibility signals and the decision method a prospective client cannot currently inspect.

### Why RN Studio?

Answered through demonstrated—not asserted—research, fact qualification, positioning, executive copy, content architecture, evidence design, and a purpose-built private experience.

### What would saying yes require?

Answered as a focused knowledge-capture conversation, privacy decisions, consolidated review cycles, and an initial thirty-minute fit conversation. The page does not imply unlimited access or a preselected fee.

## Why no additional copy change was made

The remaining conversion tension is appropriate rather than defective:

- Moving price onto the page would require an approved commercial offer that does not yet exist.
- Promising output quantities before learning Mark’s objectives would turn a strategy engagement into a commodity package.
- Adding a form would create data handling and privacy obligations without improving the intended “reply to the sender” path.
- Adding urgency, scarcity, testimonials, projected leads, or ROI would manufacture evidence.
- Removing the independent-work and proposal labels would make the experience more misleading, not more persuasive.
- Adding more biography would slow the transition from résumé facts to the differentiated value of Mark’s judgment.

2. Introduce it in one or two sentences; do not provide a second pitch that competes with the page.
3. Describe it as an independent strategic demonstration, not a redesign Mark requested.
4. Avoid promising traffic, leads, revenue, ranking, or publication outcomes.
5. Confirm the deployed response retains `noindex`, `nofollow`, private/no-store caching, and no route leakage.
