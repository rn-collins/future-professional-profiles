# Individualized content-engine specification

Research baseline: 2 September 2026  
Status: editorial and product specification for a speculative professional-presence prototype

## Publication boundary

The biographies summarize the sourced public record. Featured modules and posts are proposed future editorial—not evidence that either subject wrote, published, approved, commissioned, or performed them. A publishable client program would require subject review, transaction-specific substantiation, image clearance, and replacement of every modeled interface signal with real data.

No engagement figure, testimonial, partnership, client outcome, or transaction result is supplied by the research record. The content data therefore stores zero engagement and labels every post `Proposed post`.

## Evidence behavior

Every proposed post carries three distinct objects:

1. **Status** — identifies the artifact as proposed future content.
2. **Strategy** — explains the communication job the post is designed to perform.
3. **Evidence** — links the public source supporting the professional premise behind the idea.

The evidence link does not prove every analytical sentence in a proposed post. It proves only the underlying credential, role, or public activity from which the editorial concept was responsibly derived.

## Mark H. Young engine

### Position

**The advisor who sees beneath the listing.**

Mark’s public biography supports a coherent authority stack: Kāneʻohe upbringing, Hawaiʻi education, finance training, early commercial and residential appraisal/analysis, current brokerage leadership, professional designations, broad client work, and an extensive company-published recognition record. The engine converts that record into visible decision quality.

### Core transformation

`Unphotogenic fact → decision consequence → disciplined question → qualified conversation`

The content should begin where ordinary property marketing tends to stop. A beautiful feature becomes the entry point, never the conclusion. Condition, basis, insurance, sequencing, renovation economics, negotiation, and the client’s real priority supply the substance.

### Series portfolio

| Series | Audience job | Primary format | Evidence rule | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What the Listing Doesn’t Tell You | Help buyers and investors identify consequential omissions | Annotated carousel / field video | Cite the relevant public rule, report, document, or credential; never imply inspection without one | Property-specific diligence conversation |
| Decision Before Transaction | Help families sequence a sale, purchase, financing, and move | Decision tree / scenario note | Scenarios must be explicitly anonymized, composite, or hypothetical | Buyer or seller strategy session |
| Oʻahu, Without the Theater | Replace generic market recitation with decision-level interpretation | Monthly briefing / chart post | Link the underlying Honolulu Board of REALTORS® release and date-stamp every figure | Subscription or referral-partner response |
| Inside the Negotiation | Explain preparation and tradeoffs without revealing clients | Field note / audio-over visual | Do not claim a result unless transaction evidence and client permission exist | Complex-case inquiry |

### Voice constraints

- Calm authority over superlatives.
- Concrete questions over generic advice.
- Place names only when the analysis is genuinely place-specific.
- “Here is what would change my answer” is more credible than false certainty.
- Recognition supports the standard; it must never become the story’s only substance.

## Samuel “Sam” Wolff engine

### Position

**The systems translator for land, energy, policy, and return.**

Sam’s public record does not support a frictionless, fully verified employer chronology. It does support a distinctive set of intersections: water technology, residential solar development, municipal neighborhood administration, publicly indexed commercial-property advising, and current neighborhood-board service. The content engine makes that systems pattern useful without manufacturing continuity.

### Core transformation

`Public or infrastructure fact → affected system → private consequence → more honest model`

The content should reconnect variables commonly treated as external context—energy, water, permitting, transportation, public safety, access, community legitimacy—to the decisions that owners, investors, and residents actually experience.

### Series portfolio

| Series | Audience job | Primary format | Evidence rule | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Place Belongs in the Model | Help investors understand place as an asset fundamental | System map / field essay | Distinguish sourced fact from Sam’s proposed analysis on screen | Advisory or cross-sector conversation |
| Public Meeting, Private Consequence | Translate civic records into owner and resident implications | Source annotation / short explainer | Attribute board actions to the board; attribute personal positions only when directly documented | Stakeholder dialogue or subscription |
| Change the Asset, Change the Questions | Prepare residential investors for commercial-property risk | Framework carousel / scenario memo | Retain the current-role verification boundary until first-party confirmation | Commercial-transition assessment |
| Energy Is Property Strategy | Connect infrastructure choices to cost and resilience | Visual case framework / interview | Use documented RevoluSun experience; do not imply current solar practice | Energy–property collaboration |

### Voice constraints

- Curious, systems-literate, and unafraid of unresolved tradeoffs.
- Translate institutional language without flattening it.
- Name who bears a cost, delay, loss of access, or risk.
- Never present a neighborhood-board position as Sam’s personal view without direct attribution.
- Preserve the distinction between documented experience and the strategic interpretation built from it.

## Dynamic strategy-layer behavior

The interface should allow a viewer to change audiences without changing the factual record. Each audience view reveals four fields: the audience, its decision tension, the editorial move, and the intended business or public outcome. This is strategy personalization—not biographical personalization.

Recommended initial states:

- Mark: `Move-up families`
- Sam: `Residential-to-commercial`

The page may remember the viewer’s selected audience locally, but should not infer sensitive traits, silently enrich identities, or alter factual claims based on visitor data.

## Measurement contract

The prototype must not simulate social proof. Once a real program exists, measure:

- search impressions for specific decision questions;
- qualified profile visits by relevant professional category;
- saves and substantive comments per 1,000 impressions;
- evidence-link opens and briefing completion;
- newsletter subscriptions and returning readers;
- conversations attributable to a named series or question;
- referral introductions and qualified advisory inquiries;
- time from first content interaction to qualified conversation.

The operating question is not “Did this look popular?” It is “Did the right person understand the subject’s judgment well enough to begin a better conversation?”
