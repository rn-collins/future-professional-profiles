## Stop rule

Discovery stopped when additional broad searches repeated four stable result families—official records, brokerage explainers, institutional market reports, and generic national CRE guidance—without adding consequential identity-resolved facts. The remaining high-value evidence is first-party: subject interviews, audience interviews, approved analytics, current-role confirmation, and controlled case material.
