## Market findings shaping both profiles

- Oʻahu’s 2026 residential market is segmented rather than simply “hot” or “cold”: recent board data shows stronger competition for single-family homes than condos and meaningful price-band variation.
- Current buyer attention clusters around affordability, insurance, condition, competition, and whether a property’s basis is defensible.
- Oʻahu commercial fundamentals reward specificity: industrial vacancy remains exceptionally tight while office conditions and investment narratives differ.
- Hawaiʻi content competitors frequently publish undifferentiated listing tours and monthly-stat recitations. Both subjects need interpretation, not more inventory reposting.
- Search visibility increasingly rewards question-led, locally specific explanations that can be summarized accurately by search and answer engines.

## Measurement model

Do not use fabricated engagement as a performance claim. In a live client program, measure:

- Qualified profile views and audience-role mix
- Saves and substantive comments per 1,000 impressions
- Direct inquiries attributable to a specific content theme
- Referral-partner conversations

- Newsletter opt-ins and returning readers
- Content-assisted listings, advisory calls, or investment conversations
- Time from first content touch to qualified conversation
