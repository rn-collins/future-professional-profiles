# Public-source census

Research date: 2 September 2026  
Purpose: document what was searched, what was found, and where a truthful limit remains.

## Coverage method

The review covered first-party biographies and media libraries; professional and licensing directories; current and archived property listings; company and publication indexes; City and County of Honolulu board pages, agendas, summaries, testimony, and document archives; local news; university and appointment coverage; public subject-controlled social profiles; search-engine indexes for otherwise inaccessible professional profiles; and current residential/commercial market reporting.

This is a deep public-web census, not a claim that every public record in existence was retrieved. Records behind authentication, paywalls, unindexed databases, deleted pages, private accounts, and ambiguous same-name results remain outside the verified set.
