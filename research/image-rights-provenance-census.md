## Required rights packet

For each production image, store:

1. original file supplied by the rights holder;
2. creator/photographer name and credit line;
3. copyright owner;
4. written permission or license terms;
5. permitted media, territory, duration, cropping/editing and paid-promotion scope;
6. subject/model releases and guardian consent when applicable;
7. property/client privacy approval where applicable;
8. source URL, retrieval date, original filename, dimensions and checksum;
9. approved alt text and focal crop;
10. expiration/revocation/takedown contact and replacement plan.

## Manipulation boundary

Do not use generative fill, face swap, synthetic relighting, “beautification,” family-member removal, or undocumented AI upscaling on documentary portraits. Ordinary responsive resizing and an approved crop may be acceptable, but record the derivative and never describe reconstructed pixels as the original. The technically clean solution to a sharper Sam portrait is a subject-supplied original or approved new portrait.
