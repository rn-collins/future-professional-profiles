## Evidence grades

- **A — primary/institutional:** government record, direct testimony, subject-controlled profile, or institutional record.
- **B — named reporting:** reputable publication with direct attribution or a reported appointment.
- **C — corroborated index:** matching professional/search index, but source page is incomplete or authentication-gated.
- **D — discovery lead:** aggregator or search snippet; never sufficient alone for profile copy.
- **X — excluded:** identity mismatch, private/sensitive material, unverifiable claim, or insufficient attribution.
