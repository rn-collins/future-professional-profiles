## Recognition-methodology reconciliation

### Aloha ʻĀina

The [Honolulu Board of REALTORS® awards page](https://www.hicentral.com/awards-programs.php) says the program began in 1998, honors REALTORS® who excel professionally and serve clients, and accepts nominations only from the public at large. Mark’s company page lists multiple years as “Aloha Aina Nominee.” Safe language is therefore **company-published record of public nominations**. It is not safe to call every listed year an independently verified win, production rank, or audited customer-satisfaction result.

### HONOLULU Magazine

The [2013 Homebuyers’ Guide](https://www.honolulumagazine.com/2013-homebuyers-guide/) says selection was editorial, not purchasable, and used a Ward Research survey of more than 10,000 new buyers, real-estate professionals and magazine readers, asking for recommendations and customer-service/satisfaction rankings. This supports the integrity of the **2013** method. The retrieved 2015, 2016, 2018, 2019 and 2022 pages establish the continuing directory/feature ecosystem but did not each restate methodology. Do not silently project the 2013 method across all later years.

### Real Producers and brokerage “Top Producer”

The Honolulu publisher/social archive supports Mark’s October 2020 cover/top-producer framing. A Real Producers page for another market says top agents there are selected by MLS sales volume; that cannot establish the Honolulu edition’s exact threshold, period or method. True Real Estate’s annual “Top Producer Award” is company-published and no internal calculation or denominator was retrieved. Both should remain attributed, dated recognition—not converted into universal rankings.
